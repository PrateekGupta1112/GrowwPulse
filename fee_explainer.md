# Customer Fee Explainer: Why can a trade cost more than the brokerage?

- **Brokerage is only one line item.** A trade may also include applicable statutory and exchange-related charges; the exact set depends on the transaction and product.
- **Check the current pricing page before trading.** Rates and applicable charges can change; use Groww’s official pricing page rather than assuming one flat fee applies to every trade.
- **DP charges are distinct from brokerage.** Depository-related charges may apply to delivery sells; check the official help article for when they are levied.
- **Review the contract note/transaction details.** Compare each listed charge and its calculation with the official pricing information.
- **For a specific unexpected debit,** use the in-app support channel and share the order/contract-note details; do not infer the reason from the total debit alone.
- **Last checked:** 19 Oct 2026. This is general information, not investment advice.
